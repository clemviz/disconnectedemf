Why care?

Humans are electromagnetic by nature. EMFs are everywhere and interact with our bodies.

Unexplained headaches, fatigue, stress, disturbed sleep, prickling or burning skin. These are the complaints people most often report, and chronic exposure to artificial electromagnetic fields is one of the causes worth ruling out.

What an EMF actually is

EMF (ElectroMagnetic Field) is an invisible physical field made of two components: electric and magnetic. They vary in frequency and intensity.

Read the basics

Thermal is only the tip

Official limits are built on heating effects. The Bio-Initiative meta-analysis of some 2,000 studies documents non-thermal ones: sleep, immune function, oxidative stress, DNA damage.

See the evidence

Electrosensitivity is real

The WHO recognises EHS symptoms as real and classifies RF radiation from 30 kHz to 300 GHz as a possible human carcinogen (Group 2B). It appears to build with the dose.

The assessment

An assessment in four steps

Disconnected offers a full assessment of the electromagnetic environment in your home. We measure peak and average values for electric and magnetic fields, radiofrequencies, and check for BPL and earthing issues.

  • Identification of the emission sources, internal and external
  • Measurement of the intensity area by area: living room, office, bedrooms, kitchen
  • Protective and preventive solutions, tested on site wherever possible
  • Advice and tips to reduce your exposure in daily life

What the assessment covers

1

Survey

We walk the property and locate what is emitting: wiring, appliances, routers, DECT bases, meters, and masts or neighbours outside.

2

Measure

Low-frequency electric and magnetic fields, radiofrequency power density and peaks, plus BPL and earthing checks, recorded room by room.

3

Solve

Solutions are trialled in place so you can see the reading fall before you commit to anything. Free changes come before paid ones.

4

Report

A summary assessment report with results per area and recommendations is always included. An in-depth report is an optional extra, as is a follow-up visit to verify the improvements in real conditions.

Standards

Measured against biology-based thresholds

National limits do not account for the biological impact of electromagnetic fields. That is why Disconnected reports against three independent standards or guidelines.

2012

Bio-Initiative

Twenty-nine authors from ten countries (MDs, PhDs and public-health researchers) re-derived threshold values intended to preserve health.

About Bio-Initiative
2015

SBM-2015

The German Institute of Building Biology’s standard, adopted for mobile telephony by the health department of the Salzburg region in Austria.

2017

IGNIR

International Guidelines on Non-Ionizing Radiation, born in Oxford and based on the EUROPAEM EMF Guidelines 2016.
