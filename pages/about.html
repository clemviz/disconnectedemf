About Disconnected

An independent practice measuring electromagnetic fields exposure in homes and providing support and solutions to reduce it. Founded by an engineer and a mental health professional.

Our approach

Measurement and tailored solutions

Disconnected exists because the exposure most of us live in is invisible, unmeasured and growing. But it's real, and it can the exposure can be reduced, often immediately with simple and free changes. We are here to help you understand your environment and make those changes.

An assessment is a measurement exercise. We record what is there, compare it against independent thresholds, and hand you a prioritised list. The first items on that list are nearly always free: move the bed away from that wall, use internet via an ethernet cable, replace the DECT base with a corded handset.

Although we offer some psychological support, we do not diagnose illness or provide medical advice. We are not a medical practice, and we do not treat patients. We are here to help you understand your environment and make changes to reduce your exposure.

What we stand on

Three principles

Independence

No affiliation with shielding manufacturers. Recommendations based on the readings, not a catalogue.

Precaution

We report against biology-based thresholds, because heat-based limits answer a different question.

People-centered

We are aware that people have different needs and sensitivities, and we tailor our approach to each individual.
