An assessment to be aware of your EMF exposure

Unexplained chronic headaches, fatigue, stress, sleep disturbance, prickling or burning skin? It could be an intolerance to artificial electromagnetic waves. The assessment makes the invisible visible.

Why measure

Prevention is better than cure

Electromagnetic hypersensitivity seems to be triggered when the organism widely loses its capacity to defend itself against these invisible aggressions, a dose effect. So even if you feel all right and are simply asking questions about your exposure at home, an assessment lets you understand your electromagnetic environment and put efficient protection in place for yourself and the people you live with.

As the world digitalises, the electromagnetic fog thickens. Exposure keeps rising in time and intensity, and home working has pushed more of it into the rooms we live in. The good news: easy solutions exist to reduce EMF at home, and many of them cost nothing.

Symptoms people bring to us

  • Chronic headaches
  • Fatigue on waking
  • Difficulty falling asleep
  • Waking at night
  • Stress and irritability
  • Prickling or burning skin
  • Poor concentration
  • Tinnitus

None of these are proof of an EMF problem on their own. Measuring is how you rule the question in or out instead of guessing.

The method

An assessment in four steps

Disconnected offers a full assessment of your electromagnetic environment at home.

1

Sources

Identification of the emission sources, internal and external, from the masts nearby to the router and other devices in your home.

2

Measurement

Intensity measured area by area: living room, office, bedrooms, kitchen and anywhere you spend real time.

3

Solutions

Protective and preventive solutions suggested and, where possible, tested on site so you can see the effect before you buy anything.

4

Report

A summary assessment report with measurement results, recommendations per area and daily-life advice is always included. An in-depth report and a follow-up visit are optional extras.
